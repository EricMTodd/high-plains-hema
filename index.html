High Plains HEMA Collective

We are a collective of self-driven individuals with peer-lead classes focusing on the enjoyment of learning various Historical European sword arts in an inclusive, safe, and fun environment.

Our main focus for weapon systems is Joachim Meyers longsword, a 16th century German Cutler, freifechter, freighter, fencing master, but we also train Meyers polearms, Iberian zwiehanders, dussack, and Lichtenhauer longsword.

Our classes are small and personal, with practices taking place on Wednesdays and Saturdays.
